# 真题错题归因分析 — 标准样本 (Error Analysis Archetype)

> 本文件作为 `06-wiki/analyses/` 下的标准分析样本，展示如何将一道六级仔细阅读真题拆解为可复用的得分规律。

---

## 样本案例：仔细阅读“实验/学者观点”题型

### 1. 原题重现
- **题干**：What do critics say about the widespread application of automated hiring algorithms?
- **原文定位段**：
  > *Proponents of automated hiring tools argue that algorithms can process thousands of resumes in seconds, eliminating human fatigue. **However, critics point out that because these models are trained on historical corporate data, they inadvertently replicate and even amplify preexisting institutional prejudices against underrepresented applicants.***
- **四个选项**：
  - A) It significantly reduces the workload of human resource departments.
  - B) It fails to screen out unqualified candidates effectively.
  - C) It tends to perpetuate and worsen existing workplace biases.
  - D) It lacks sufficient historical data to make accurate predictions.

### 2. 破题定位与同义改写映射
- **定位锚点**：题干 `critics` + `automated hiring algorithms` $\rightarrow$ 直接锁定原文 `However, critics point out that...`。
- **同义替换链（正解 C）**：
  - `replicate`（复制/重现） $\Longleftrightarrow$ **`perpetuate`**（使延续/使永久化）
  - `amplify`（放大/加剧） $\Longleftrightarrow$ **`worsen`**（恶化/加剧）
  - `preexisting institutional prejudices`（既有的制度性偏见） $\Longleftrightarrow$ **`existing workplace biases`**（现有的职场偏见）

### 3. 干扰项设坑手法解剖
- **A 项（答非所问/张冠李戴）**：对应第一句 `Proponents`（支持者）的观点，而非题干问的 `critics`（批评者）。
- **B 项（无中生有）**：原文未讨论“能否筛掉不合格候选人”，属于主观臆测。
- **D 项（正反混淆/拼凑原词）**：照抄了原文的 `historical data`，但原文说的是“基于历史数据训练”，而非“缺乏足够的历史数据”。
